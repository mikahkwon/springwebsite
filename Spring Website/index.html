What Does Spring Feel Like?

scroll ↓

Spring is something you feel before you see it.

move your cursor to wipe the fog

The first day you leave the jacket at home. Sun warm on your neck while the air is still cool. Damp soil, soft new grass, petals that bruise when you touch them.

  • warmth on skin
  • a cool breeze
  • damp soil
  • soft petals

Spring feels like finally letting go.

Everything you held in all winter starts to loosen. Your body, your plans, your mood. A little restless, a little nervous, but lighter.

  • relief
  • lightness
  • restlessness
  • a fresh start
